Step-by-Step Voting Guide

1

Verification

First Polling Officer checks your name on the electoral roll and ID proof (Aadhar/Voter ID).

2

The Mark

Second Polling Officer marks your finger with indelible ink and gives you a slip.

3

The Vote

Third Officer takes the slip. You proceed to the voting compartment to press the EVM button.

4

Confirmation

The VVPAT machine displays a slip for 7 seconds showing your chosen candidate. Be sure to check it!
